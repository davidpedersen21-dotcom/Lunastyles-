The Maker

Hello, I'm Magaly

I'm a self-taught textile artist based in Long Beach, California, with a deep love for the meditative art of kantha — the ancient South Asian tradition of layering and hand-stitching fabric into something new.

Every wrap, quilt, and wearable piece I create carries its own story. I source vintage and reclaimed fabrics, giving each one a second life through slow, intentional craft.

What you wear isn't just fabric — it's hours of care, color instinct, and a belief that everyday objects should be beautiful.
