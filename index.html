Our Story

Welcome to Luna Styles ✨

I curate one-of-a-kind kantha pieces full of color, texture, and soul — from wearable art to everyday statement pieces. Alongside my curated finds, I also handcraft select items myself, including reversible bucket hats and handmade tote bags sewn with love and individuality in mind.

Expect cozy boho vibes, sustainable fashion, vibrant prints, and unique pieces you won't find twice. Thank you for supporting small business and handmade artistry 🤍
