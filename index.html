✦ The Story ✦

Hi, I'm Maggie

Welcome to Luna Styles — a small shop built on big color, sustainable fashion, and the belief that what you wear should feel like you.

I curate one-of-a-kind kantha pieces full of texture and soul — from wearable art to everyday statement pieces. Alongside my curated finds, I also handcraft select items myself, including reversible bucket hats and handmade tote bags sewn with love.

Every piece is unique. Every purchase supports a small, woman-owned, handmade business. Expect cozy boho vibes, sustainable fashion, vibrant prints, and pieces you won't find twice.
