🖼️ Luna Styles — Image Export Bookmarklet

One click on your Etsy shop page → downloads every listing's photos as a single zip file, organized into one folder per listing (named after the item title).

Step 1 — Drag this to your bookmarks bar:

🖼️ Export Images

Make sure your bookmarks bar is visible first (Ctrl+Shift+B in Chrome/Edge).

Step 2 — Use it:

  1. Open your Etsy shop: etsy.com/shop/LunaStyles2026Shop
  2. Click Export Images in your bookmarks bar
  3. Your browser may ask permission to download multiple files — click Allow
  4. Watch the status toast in the top-right corner — it walks through each listing
  5. When done, a single luna-styles-listing-images.zip file downloads
  6. Unzip it — each listing gets its own folder named after the item title, containing that listing's photos

This visits every listing page one at a time, so it can take a few minutes for a shop with dozens of listings. Don't close the tab while it's running.

Heads up — this one is experimental. Etsy's page structure and image server aren't things I can test against directly, so a few things might not work exactly as described the first time you try it:
  • If a listing's photos don't come through, Etsy's image server may be blocking the download — the toast will tell you how many images failed, and the zip will still contain everything that succeeded.
  • If nothing downloads at all, or the zip is empty, let me know exactly what the toast said and I'll adjust it.
