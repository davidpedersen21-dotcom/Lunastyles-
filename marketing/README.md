## `matrix/` — responsive aspect variants

The launch video and X social clip, fanned into all 4 standard social aspects (responsive layout, not crops):

- `launch-16x9.mp4`, `launch-9x16.mp4`, `launch-1x1.mp4`, `launch-4x5.mp4`
- `social-16x9.mp4`, `social-9x16.mp4`, `social-1x1.mp4`, `social-4x5.mp4`

Launch-video variants were re-encoded (CRF 30) to fit the 8MB web-hero size budget — Remotion's default encode came in at 15-26MB, too heavy to embed/autoplay on a landing page.

## `thumbs/` — poster stills

One representative JPG per aspect (`thumb-16x9.jpg`, `thumb-9x16.jpg`, `thumb-1x1.jpg`, `thumb-4x5.jpg`), pulled from the matrix renders. Use as a `<video poster>` or static fallback.

## `postkit/` — paste-ready per-platform folders

Each folder has the right-aspect video (where available), a thumbnail, a lint-gated `caption.txt`, `alt.txt`, and a `POST.md` checklist:

- `x/` — social-16x9.mp4 + caption/alt/checklist
- `linkedin/` — launch-16x9.mp4 + caption/alt/checklist
- `youtube/` — launch-16x9.mp4 + caption/alt/checklist
- `tiktok/`, `shorts/`, `instagram/` — thumb + caption/alt/checklist only (video omitted, see gap below)

## Known gaps

- **Audio**: `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` in the engine repo's `.env` was rejected (401 invalid key) when generating the music track. Every video above ships **silent** — a documented valid fallback, but not what was intended (music-only was requested). To add music: fix the key, then in the engine repo run `node scripts/build-lunastyles-audio.mjs` followed by `node scripts/merge-launch-audio.mjs lunastyles`, then re-render the launch video and re-run the export matrix.
- **Captions / muted-autoplay variants**: because there's no audio yet, the `tiktok/`, `shorts/`, and `instagram/` postkit folders don't have a video (they need the audio-burned-caption variant, which requires the VO/music manifest). Once audio is added, re-run `node scripts/render-matrix.mjs lunastyles` (captioned 9:16/1:1 variants) and `node scripts/build-captions.mjs lunastyles`, then `node scripts/build-postkit.mjs lunastyles` again.

## Brand

`brands/lunastyles.json` in the engine repo. Palette derived from the live site's kantha color system (crimson `#B01030` primary, kantha-yellow `#D4A020` accent/CTA-text, warm cream `#FFF8EE` base). Fonts: Playfair Display (display), Crimson Pro (body), Space Mono (mono/labels).
